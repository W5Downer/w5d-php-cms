### 3.2 What the project layer tells us about W5D’s maturity

The site is **status-transparent to the point of self-limitation**: it publishes no reviews yet (“Awaiting the first review”), says the domain is not purchased, and distinguishes live/operational from draft/concept. This is consistent with the dossier’s own “Sidq” principle: **no page asserts more than its evidence supports** .

The project register therefore reveals a **three-speed organisation**:

1. **Live/operational**: MetaX Academy, Ontology Department, Studio, 2030B, 26 developers, 16 departments.
2. **Concept/draft**: KernID, SelfAI, كن أعلم, WebBooks.
3. **Design ceiling**: the 130-service, 1,000-per-service architecture described in the dossier — a target state, not current headcount.

This distinction is crucial: the dossier’s 130,000-member design equation is **not** a claim that 130,000 experts are currently active. The site’s “26 developers (Tunisia)” and “5 programmes” are the real delivery footprint today.
